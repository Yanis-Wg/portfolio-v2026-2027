Description

During my second year in MMI, I had the opportunity to complete a six-month internship with a group named Expert IT. In this group, I worked for several companies, primarily as a web developer. I created four websites, each built from scratch by myself. One was for Streamcore, another for Brain Networks, a third for Expert IT itself, and the last one for Connect 3C. Additionally, thanks to my skills, I took the initiative to assist as a graphic designer for a special project: the creation of a kakemono for Streamcore. This internship taught me a great deal about working independently, requiring extensive research on the internet and utilizing educational resources on platforms like OpenClassrooms and YouTube. In addition to delivering these websites, I completed them quickly without sacrificing quality. I demonstrated to myself that I am a reactive, dynamic, rigorous, and autonomous individual.
